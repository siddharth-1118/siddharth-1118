### 📂 Featured Architectures & Projects

#### 🏛️ Campus Portals & Academic Ecosystem
*   **[Academia Nexus](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/academia-nexus):** A sleek, futuristic 3D dashboard for SRM Academia built with Next.js and Three.js.
*   **[Classivo3](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/classivo3):** A premium academic companion app for SRM students tracking attendance, marks, and timetables.
*   **[SRM AI Study Hub](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/srm-ai-study-hub):** An AI-powered platform providing structured, subject-wise materials and PYQs.

#### 🤖 AI OS & Custom Languages
*   **SIGNAL (Priority AI OS):** A complex backend architecture designed to aggregate, score, and prioritize communications.
*   **[Creatorlang](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/creatorlang):** A unified custom syntax language engineered to generate images, videos, and 3D models.
*   **[VSS-Lang](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/vss-lang):** An experimental programming language utilizing Telugu-style English keywords.

*   **[Face_Unlock](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/face_unlock):** Command-based biometric authentication system.
*   **[Air_Draw](https://github.com/siddharth-1118/air_draw):** A platform for drawing in mid-air using real-time hand tracking.
